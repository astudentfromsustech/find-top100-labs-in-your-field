The genome sequence of the Black-veined White butterfly, Aporia crataegi (Linnaeus, 1758)co-author

2025-02-21 · Wellcome Open Research · 0 citations · doi:10.12688/wellcomeopenres.17709.2

We present a genome assembly from an individual male Aporia crataegi (the black-veined white; Arthropoda; Insecta; Lepidoptera; Pieridae). The genome sequence is 230 megabases in span. The complete assembly is scaffolded into 26 chromosomal pseudomolecules, with the Z sex chromosome assembled. Gene annotation of this assembly on Ensembl has identified 10,860 protein coding genes.

The genome sequence of the Essex Skipper butterfly, Thymelicus lineola (Ochsenheimer, 1808)first author

2024-08-12 · Wellcome Open Research · 3 citations · doi:10.12688/wellcomeopenres.22847.1

We present a genome assembly from an individual female Essex Skipper butterfly, Thymelicus lineola (Arthropoda; Insecta; Lepidoptera; Hesperiidae). The genome sequence spans 511.80 megabases. Most of the assembly is scaffolded into 30 chromosomal pseudomolecules, including the Z and W sex chromosomes. The mitochondrial genome has also been assembled and is 17.24 kilobases in length.
