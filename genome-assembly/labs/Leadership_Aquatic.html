The chromosomal genome sequence of the blushing star coral, Stephanocoenia intersepta (Lamarck, 1816) (Scleractinia: Astrocoeniidae) and its associated microbial metagenome sequenceslast author

2026-09-21 · Wellcome Open Research · 0 citations · doi:10.12688/wellcomeopenres.27573.1

We present a genome assembly from a specimen of Stephanocoenia intersepta (blushing star coral; Cnidaria; Anthozoa; Scleractinia; Astrocoeniidae). The assembly contains two haplotypes with total lengths of 656.21 megabases and 656.55 megabases. Most of haplotype 1 (94.84%) is scaffolded into 13 chromosomal pseudomolecules. Haplotype 2 was assembled to scaffold level. The mitochondrial genome has also been assembled, with a length of 19.46 kilobases. Gene annotation of this assembly by Ensembl identified 26,627 protein-coding genes. From the metagenome data, we recovered five bins, of which one was a high-quality metagenome-assembled genome.

Chromosome-level genome sequences of the elephant ear sponge, Ianthella basta (Pallas, 1766), its Haplosyllis basticola worm cobiont, and associated microbial metagenome sequenceslast author

2026-09-15 · Wellcome Open Research · 0 citations · doi:10.12688/wellcomeopenres.27510.1

We present chromosome-level genome assemblies from a specimen of Ianthella basta (Porifera; Demospongiae; Verongiida; Ianthellidae) and its Haplosyllis basticola worm cobiont (Annelida; Polychaeta; Phyllodocida; Syllidae). The I. basta assembly has a total length of 209.27 megabases, with 98.55% scaffolded into 23 chromosomal pseudomolecules, and includes a 21.23-kilobase mitochondrial genome. Gene annotation of this assembly by Ensembl identified 21,073 protein-coding genes. The H. basticola primary assembly spans 2.63 gigabases in 34 scaffolds, including eight chromosomal pseudomolecules, and has a scaffold N50 of 308.67 megabases; its mitochondrial genome is 16.48 kilobases long. From the sponge metagenome data, we recovered four bins, of which two were high-quality metagenome-assembled genomes.

The chromosomal genome sequence of the horned sponge, Axinella damicornis (Esper, 1794) (Axinellida: Axinellidae)last author

2026-08-21 · Wellcome Open Research · 0 citations · doi:10.12688/wellcomeopenres.27385.1

We present a genome assembly from a specimen of Axinella damicornis (horned sponge; Porifera; Demospongiae; Axinellida; Axinellidae). The genome sequence has a total length of 234.88 megabases. Most of the assembly (95.5%) is scaffolded into 14 chromosomal pseudomolecules. The mitochondrial genome has also been assembled, with a length of 41.17 kilobases. From the metagenome data, we recovered 32 bins, of which nine were high-quality MAGs. MAGs were largely affiliated with the Gammaproteobacteria, resembling the microbial signature of a typical low microbial abundance sponge.
