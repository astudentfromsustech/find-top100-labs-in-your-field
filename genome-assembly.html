Method

Adapted from U.S. News Best Global Universities (reputation 25 %, citation excellence 42.5 %, citations 7.5 %, output 15 %) and QS (academic reputation 30 %, citations 20 %) with per-lab proxies: GitHub adoption stands in for reputation; OpenAlex citation_normalized_percentile gives the field- and year-normalized top-10 % / top-1 % counts; PIs are matched by anchor papers, never by name search. Consortium papers inflate every co-author and stars favour general-purpose tools — the score orders labs for a tracker, it is not a quality judgement. Sources and code: knowledge_base/04_Records/research/ranking.tsv, rank_labs.py.
